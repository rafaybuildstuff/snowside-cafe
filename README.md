Game Name: Snowside Cafe

Description: In this game you start with a small empty shop with almost nothing inside and your goal is to turn it into the most popular packed cafe in the whole town. You begin with basic gear and zero hype but as you serve customers and earn money you get to buy better equipment expand your shop space and upgrade the whole place. You go from running a quiet little corner spot to building the biggest coolest hangout in town.

Playable URL: https://rafaybuildstuff.itch.io/snowside-cafe

CONTROLS
-WSAD / Arrow keys to move
-E to prepare food and upgrade

Project Structure
-assets (Has all the visuals assets used across the game)
-music (Has all the music and sfx)
-scene (has all the scenes)

Gameplay
Phase 1: you enter your restaurant for the first time
Phase 2: You start Serving your initial customers
Phase 3: You make your first upgrade (another table)
Phase 4: you unlock more area
